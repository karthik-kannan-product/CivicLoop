Known limitations

  • Local simulation only; no live Eventbrite, Iterable, LinkedIn, authentication, or observability integration.
  • Handles one campaign-prep loop and six seeded synthetic eval cases, not every nonprofit event workflow.
  • Email and social drafts are simple template text, not production creative.
  • Requires event and segment data in the embedded prototype format.
  • Cannot send, publish, schedule, change prices, create discounts, or create audience segments.
